What is makhana?

Makhana, also known as fox nuts, is an edible seed harvested from the aquatic plant Euryale ferox, which grows in ponds and wetlands. It is a source of plant-based protein, dietary fibre and essential minerals. Naturally gluten-free and low in fat, makhana is a versatile, wholesome snack that can be roasted and seasoned for a delicious, crunchy treat.

Buying makhana in Singapore

When buying makhana, decide whether you want raw makhana to roast and season at home or roasted makhana that is ready to eat. Check the pack size and packaging condition, and choose raw makhana if you want to control the roasting and seasoning yourself.

LittlePops offers premium raw makhana in a convenient 200g pack in Singapore. Because it is raw, roast it in a pan until crisp and add your preferred seasoning before eating.

Keep the unopened pack in a cool, dry place away from moisture. After opening, reseal it well and keep it dry. If you roast a batch, let it cool completely before storing it in an airtight container to help preserve its crunch.
