The simple guide to makhana (fox nuts).

Raw makhana is a versatile pantry staple. Here’s how to turn it into a crisp, seasoned snack at home — plus a few simple tips for choosing, preparing and storing it.

What is makhana?

Makhana, also called fox nuts, is the edible seed of the Euryale ferox plant. It is widely used in Indian cooking and snacking. Raw makhana has a light, mild flavour and a firm texture; dry-roasting it makes the pieces crisp and ready for seasoning.

Raw vs roasted: LittlePops Raw Makhana is sold unroasted. It is meant to be roasted at home before eating as a crunchy snack.

How to roast raw makhana

1. Heat the pan

Warm a little olive oil or ghee in a heavy-bottomed pan over low to medium heat.

2. Add makhana

Add makhana, toss to coat lightly, and spread in one layer.

3. Roast slowly

Stir regularly over low to medium heat until crisp, without burning.

4. Season

Cool slightly, then season with salt and pepper, chilli or chaat masala.

Tip: Let one piece cool and taste it. If it is still chewy, roast a little longer. Olive oil or ghee helps the seasoning stick.

Easy ways to enjoy makhana

Simple salted

Roast, add salt and enjoy.

Spiced

Add chilli, pepper, cumin or chaat masala.

With recipes

Mix roasted makhana into homemade snack mixes.

Make your own blend

Start mild and adjust the spices to taste.

Buying makhana in Singapore

When buying makhana, decide whether you want raw makhana to roast and season at home or roasted makhana that is ready to eat. Check the pack size, keep the product protected from moisture, and store it in a cool, dry place after purchase.

LittlePops currently offers premium raw makhana in a convenient 200g pack in Singapore. Because it is raw, roast it in a pan until crisp and add your preferred seasoning before eating.

How to store raw makhana

Keep the unopened pack in a cool, dry place away from moisture. After opening, reseal it well and keep it dry. If you roast a batch, let it cool completely before storing it in an airtight container. This helps prevent trapped steam from softening the snack.
