The simple guide to makhana (fox nuts).

Raw makhana is a versatile pantry staple. Here’s how to turn it into a crisp, seasoned snack at home — plus a few simple tips for choosing, preparing and storing it.

What is makhana?

Makhana, also called fox nuts, is the edible seed of the Euryale ferox plant. It is widely used in Indian cooking and snacking. Raw makhana has a light, mild flavour and a firm texture; dry-roasting it makes the pieces crisp and ready for seasoning.

Raw vs roasted: LittlePops Raw Makhana is sold unroasted. It is meant to be roasted at home before eating as a crunchy snack.

How to roast raw makhana

1. Heat the pan

Place a heavy-bottomed pan over low to medium heat and add a small amount of olive oil or ghee.

2. Add makhana

Add the raw makhana and toss well so the pieces are lightly coated. Spread them into a single layer for even roasting.

3. Roast slowly

Stir or toss regularly and roast until the makhana becomes crisp. Use low to medium heat so it roasts evenly without burning.

4. Season

Let it cool slightly, then add your favourite seasoning. Try salt and pepper, chilli, chaat masala, or a simple spice blend.

Quick texture test: Take one piece out and let it cool for a moment. It should feel crisp when you bite into it. If it is still chewy, continue roasting on low heat. A little olive oil or ghee helps the makhana roast evenly and carry the seasoning.

Easy ways to enjoy makhana

Simple salted

Roast, add a little salt and enjoy warm or cooled.

Spiced

Try chilli powder, pepper, cumin or chaat masala for a more savoury snack.

With recipes

Roasted makhana can be added to snack mixes and other homemade recipes.

Make your own blend

Start with a mild seasoning and adjust the spices to suit your taste.

How to store raw makhana

Keep the unopened pack in a cool, dry place away from moisture. After opening, reseal it well and keep it dry. If you roast a batch, allow it to cool completely before storing it in an airtight container so trapped steam does not soften the snack.
