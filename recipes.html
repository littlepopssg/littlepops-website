Classic Himalayan Salt Makhana

A simple, buttery crunch with salt and freshly cracked black pepper.

Read recipe

Yield

About 2 snack portions.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups raw LittlePops makhana
  • 1–2 tsp ghee or neutral oil (use oil for a dairy-free version)
  • Salt, to taste
  • ¼ tsp black pepper, or to taste

Method

  1. Warm a heavy pan on low-medium heat.
  2. Dry-roast makhana for 5–8 minutes, stirring often, until crisp.
  3. Add ghee or neutral oil and toss for another minute.
  4. Season with salt and pepper. Cool briefly before serving.

Peri-Peri Makhana

A spicy, tangy snack for movie nights and afternoon cravings.

Read recipe

Yield

About 2 snack portions.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups raw LittlePops makhana
  • 1 tsp neutral oil or ghee (use oil for a dairy-free/vegan version)
  • ½–1 tsp peri-peri seasoning
  • A pinch of salt, if needed

Method

  1. Dry-roast makhana over low-medium heat for 5–8 minutes until crisp.
  2. Add oil or ghee and toss to coat lightly.
  3. Turn off the heat, add peri-peri seasoning and mix well.
  4. Let cool before storing in an airtight container.

Masala Makhana

Warm Indian spices turn plain makhana into a flavour-packed bite.

Read recipe

Yield

About 2 snack portions.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups raw LittlePops makhana
  • 1 tsp ghee or neutral oil (use oil for a dairy-free/vegan version)
  • ¼ tsp turmeric
  • ½ tsp roasted cumin powder
  • ½ tsp chaat masala, plus salt to taste

Method

  1. Dry-roast makhana for 5–8 minutes, stirring often.
  2. Add ghee and toss until lightly coated.
  3. Lower the heat; add turmeric and cumin.
  4. Turn off the heat, add chaat masala and mix. Taste before adding extra salt.

Curry Leaf Makhana

A fragrant South Indian-inspired variation with curry leaves and pepper.

Read recipe

Yield

About 2 snack portions.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups raw LittlePops makhana
  • 1–2 tsp ghee or neutral oil (use oil for a dairy-free/vegan version)
  • 8–10 curry leaves, washed and thoroughly dried
  • ¼ tsp black pepper
  • Salt, to taste

Method

  1. Dry-roast makhana until crisp; transfer to a bowl.
  2. In the same pan, warm ghee and fry the fully dried curry leaves until crisp.
  3. Turn off the heat. Add pepper and salt.
  4. Toss the roasted makhana with the seasoned ghee and crushed curry leaves.

Makhana Kheer

A creamy Indian dessert with milk, cardamom and a little crunch.

Read recipe

Yield

About 3–4 small dessert servings.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup raw LittlePops makhana
  • 3 cups dairy milk, or unsweetened plant milk such as coconut, almond or oat milk for a vegan version
  • 1–2 tbsp sugar for a lightly sweet kheer; add more to taste, or sweeten with finely chopped dates or a little jaggery
  • ¼ tsp cardamom powder
  • Chopped nuts and saffron, optional

Method

  1. Dry-roast makhana for a few minutes, then cool and roughly crush half.
  2. Bring milk to a gentle simmer in a saucepan.
  3. Add makhana and simmer gently for 15–20 minutes, stirring often.
  4. Add sugar and cardamom; cook until creamy. Garnish if desired and serve warm or chilled.

Chocolate-Coated Makhana

A fun sweet snack with crisp makhana and a chocolate coating.

Read recipe

Yield

About 2 snack portions.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups raw LittlePops makhana
  • 80–100 g chocolate or chocolate chips (choose dairy-free chocolate for a vegan version)
  • ½ tsp coconut oil, optional, to help thin the melted chocolate

Method

  1. Dry-roast makhana until crisp, then let it cool completely.
  2. Melt chocolate gently in short microwave bursts or over a double boiler.
  3. Make sure the roasted makhana is completely cool before coating; warm makhana can make the chocolate melt, seize or slide off.
  4. Melt chocolate gently. For a firmer, neater set, temper the chocolate according to its package guidance, or use chocolate chips designed for melting. Stir in coconut oil only if needed, then coat the makhana.
  5. Spread on baking paper and refrigerate until fully set. Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator, especially in Singapore’s warm, humid weather.
