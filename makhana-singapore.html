Looking for makhana in Singapore?

Makhana, also known as fox nuts, is a versatile Indian snack ingredient. LittlePops makes premium raw makhana available in a convenient 200g pack for customers in Singapore.

What to look for when buying makhana

Raw or roasted?

Raw makhana gives you control over roasting, seasoning and texture at home. Roasted makhana is ready to eat.

Check the pack size

Choose a quantity that suits how often you snack and how you plan to use it. LittlePops currently offers a 200g pack.

Keep it dry

Makhana is best protected from moisture. Store the unopened pack in a cool, dry place and reseal it after opening.

Prepare it your way

Dry-roast raw makhana until crisp, then season it with salt, pepper, chilli, chaat masala or your preferred spice blend.

How to prepare it

For a simple snack, dry-roast the raw makhana in a pan over low to medium heat, stirring regularly until crisp. Let it cool slightly and add your preferred seasoning.
