Masala Makhana

Warm Indian spices turn makhana into a flavour-packed bite.

⏱ 10 minutes🍽 About 2 snack portions

Ingredients

  • 2 cups raw LittlePops makhana
  • 1 tsp ghee or neutral oil (use oil for a dairy-free/vegan version)
  • ¼ tsp turmeric
  • ½ tsp roasted cumin powder
  • ½ tsp chaat masala, plus salt to taste

Method

  1. Dry-roast makhana for 5–8 minutes, stirring often, until crisp.
  2. Add ghee or oil and toss until lightly coated.
  3. Lower the heat and add turmeric and cumin.
  4. Turn off the heat, add chaat masala and mix. Taste before adding extra salt.
