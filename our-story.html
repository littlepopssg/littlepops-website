About the Brand

LittlePops is a premium healthy snacking brand designed for the modern consumer. Rooted in natural ingredients and elevated through thoughtful design, the identity aims to balance premium quality with warmth, approachability, and everyday convenience.

Why we started LittlePops

We’re starting with premium-quality makhana, also known as fox nuts, and building a range of wholesome snacks for everyday life.

Our first product is raw makhana in a convenient 200g pack. It gives you the freedom to roast it at home, add the flavours you love and make it your own.

We believe in starting small, listening to our customers and growing one snack at a time. LittlePops is the beginning of that journey, and we’re glad you’re here.
