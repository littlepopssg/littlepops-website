Frequently asked

What is makhana?

Makhana, also called fox nuts, is a popular snack ingredient that can be roasted and seasoned.

How do I prepare raw makhana?

Dry-roast in a pan until crisp, then add your favourite seasoning. It can also be used in recipes.

How should I store it?

Keep the pack sealed and store in a cool, dry place.
