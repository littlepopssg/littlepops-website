Simple Indian snacking in Singapore.

Indian snacks can be as simple as roasting a pantry staple and adding your favourite seasoning. Here are a few easy ideas, starting with makhana.

1. Roasted makhana (fox nuts)

Makhana is a popular Indian snack ingredient that becomes crisp when roasted. Start with raw makhana, dry-roast it slowly, then season with salt, pepper, chilli, cumin or chaat masala.

For the detailed method, visit our Makhana Guide.

2. Homemade spiced snack mixes

Keep it simple

Combine roasted makhana with a small amount of your favourite nuts or seeds and season to taste.

Change the flavour

Use different spice combinations so one basic snack can become several variations.

3. Easy pantry snacks

For busy days, look for snacks with straightforward ingredients and preparation you can control at home. Reading the ingredient list and choosing portions that suit your routine can make snack planning easier.
